两份 15%，有什么分别？Why two 15% pots?

快要用的钱先存。几年内用不到的钱可以考虑投资，投资有涨有跌。Save what you’ll need soon. For money you can leave for years, consider investing. Its value can rise or fall.

EXP · 油钱多 RM100，怎样调？Petrol costs RM100 more. What can you adjust?

上班要用的油钱先留够。从可以迟点花的开心钱挪 RM100。Set aside petrol for work. Move RM100 from fun spending that can wait.
