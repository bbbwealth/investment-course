为什么有两份 15%？Why are there two 15% pots?

快要用的钱先存起来。比如明年要付的买屋头期。Save money you’ll need soon, like next year’s home down payment.

几年内用不到的钱，可以考虑投资。投资有涨有跌。Consider investing money you won’t need for years. Investments can rise or fall in value.
